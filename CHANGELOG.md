# Changelog

## v0.4.0 — 2026-11-16 · plugin 0.4.0 with methodology v3.1

Methodology v3.0 → **v3.1**; plugin v0.3.0 → **v0.4.0** — a minor plugin version, because both key changes reach the methodology's mechanisms (L38). Two problems lead the release. The engine did not keep to directions the human had given again and again, and grew stricter on its own, round after round: critics had to file a minimum number of objections, replacement wording bound everything downstream, the audit of outward material could only add qualifiers, and the rules a card proposed slid into standards nobody had approved. And model tiers were decided on the fly — moved mid-run to save quota. The sources are defects logged across several programmes since 0.3.0, among them an unattended run that kept tightening its own acceptance standards and a run that changed tiers for quota partway through. Every change below traces to a problem ID in the internal update plan (L01–L56).

Self-check before release: the methodology's v3.1 changes stood plan tribunal ST-04 (standard severity) under this release's own contracts — three isolated critics reporting faults only, with no quota, run by general-purpose agents from the new contract texts because the installed plugin was still 0.3.0: 16 objections, 12 after merging duplicates; 7 corrected, 5 rejected with reasons; no claim was weakened, so no defense round was due. Seven independent verification passes (traceability to the plan, the template blocks, weaving and the decision bar, the light items, a fresh read of every skill and agent, the scripts, leanness and privacy) and the three critics raised 53 findings, 44 after merging duplicates: 27 confirmed and fixed, 3 proposals that proved to be corrections and were applied, 5 proposals that would add rules, checks or reads recorded for a later plan and not adopted, 9 rejected. `python -m pytest -q scripts/tests`: 99 passed; all three scripts report version 0.4.0. Skill descriptions were checked for trigger collisions (none, beyond a description that names another skill's phrase to send the user there), and a personal-information scan of the repository found names only in the author byline and metadata.

Size (L41): prompt text — the `.md` files under `skills/` and `agents/` — 46,397 → 49,277 words (+2,880, +6.2%); the English methodology 10,837 → 12,121 (+1,284).

### Methodology v3.1 (docs/METHODOLOGY.md — single source; docs/METHODOLOGY_zh.md and the bilingual PDF are generated from it)

- **Correcting is free; tightening is a proposal** (§2) — a wrong fact, number, citation or derivation step is fixed without asking; weakening a claim, adding a rule, gate or check that later work must obey, or reading an open default the stricter way is a proposal the human disposes of. The conservative ratchet — restrictions, clauses or content the AI adds that neither the methodology, the charter nor the human asked for — is a named failure mode (§9). (L48, L31)
- **The U-series holds the human's standing directions** (§2) — no longer "objection classes the human has closed": each entry points to the human's own words and records the reading the human confirmed, and binds the orchestrator and every seat, not only the critics, wherever the methodology leaves a choice (the next question, an open default, what enters a record or a digest). An entry is made only when the human calls a direction standing or confirms the AI's reading; none switches off a mandatory step or leaves a wrong fact uncorrected. (L48, L55)
- **Critics report faults only, with no quota** (§3-P6, Appendix A) — receivable: a false or missing fact, number, citation or step; a conclusion its derivation does not deliver; a sentence the data or source contradicts; a passage that cannot be understood. "Should be more cautious", "add a qualifier" and wording preferences are not objections. "No receivable fault found" is a valid result; the severity dial sets how deep the critics look, never how many objections they file; anti-sycophancy rests on fresh, isolated contexts, the endorsement ban and every objection pinned to its source. (L46, L02)
- **Nearness to prior work is no reason to shrink** (§3-P6) — nearness is recorded and cited, and only SAME (prior work reached the same conclusion by the same argument) removes a claim. (L46)
- **Weakening with no fault shown needs a fresh defense first, at every tier** (§3-P6, Appendix A) — a claim about to be weakened or withdrawn on an objection that shows no wrong fact, number, citation or step first gets a fresh defense; only if that fails is it weakened, and every weakening is listed in the verdict for the human. Factual corrections skip this round. At exploratory severity the defense is handed the P7 pre-computation as data; with no deducer, a fresh defense agent argues. (L46, L05, L50)
- **Dead sentences bind; replacement wording is a suggestion** (§3-P6, §8) — a sentence the tribunal killed may not be asserted downstream; its replacement language is marked as a suggestion and the author decides; wording the human has ratified stands. The qualifier-fidelity audit stays, retargeted: it lists outward sentences that claim more than the verdict left standing — a dead sentence restated, or a number, scope or period the results do not have — and a missing qualifier is not a finding; it is also the translation-layer check. (L03, L41)
- **A card's rules stay on the card** (§3-P8) — a rule, gate, threshold or checklist item a card proposes takes one item in the decision bar (default: not adopted); the human adopts by naming the card, and adopted text replaces or merges with what it supersedes instead of being appended. A quota stop pauses a cycle, to resume after the reset. (L48, L49)
- **Gaps and one-time authorizations** (§7) — where the charter leaves a gap, an unattended run takes the reading closest to the human's own words and standing directions and records it ("stricter is safer" is not a reason); an authorization covers only the items in front of the human when it was given. (L48)
- **Question selection and the cycle cadence** (§3-P8, Appendix D.2) — "highest priority" is the human's run queue, then the frozen plan's next open line, then the seed that most directly advances the Vision. Every M cycles (default 4) a script checks the registry: if the last M questions all came from salvage or earlier cards, the next comes from the queue, the plan or the Vision, and the handoff says so first — it steers, never stops. (L48, L56)
- **Model tiers by the nature of the work, fixed before the run** (§7, Appendix D.1) — new derivation, the gate, and architecture, synthesis and final drafting follow the session's model (`inherit`, the human's tactical choice); reading, retrieval, checking, fidelity, blind reading, bookkeeping, preflight, mechanical revision of a gate-passed derivation and all three critics, at every severity, run at the next-strongest tier (`opus`). Once a run starts no tier changes because of quota, up or down; finished work is never redone; only an explicit instruction from the human changes a tier; the gate is never below the table. (L49, L24)
- **A usage fence** (§7, Appendix D.2–D.3) — an unattended run is bounded by the account's all-models weekly quota: at 80% it winds down (the handoff updated first, the item in hand finished, nothing new opened — in the Forge, the candidates already submitted are gated and no deducer is added); at 100%, or at any platform limit, it stops and the human resets and resumes. "Run it full" at launch skips the wind-down; "wind down at X%" sets X. An unfilled per-round submission cap takes its suggested value. (L49, L19, L21)
- **Rolling handoff and resume** (§7) — before every dispatch batch the run's resume line in the log is overwritten with what has landed, what is in flight and what comes next, so a stop at any moment loses nothing; the next "run G2G" resumes an unclosed run before anything else. (L49, L20)
- **One decision file** (§7) — every open decision lives in one overwrite-style file, `g2g/05_plans/decision-bar.md`, added the moment it is queued and deleted once ruled, its ruling first copied verbatim to where it takes effect. The four-line format keeps its name; its first line is now the *background* (where the item came from and what the AI did), and a *where to look* line follows for checking. (L01, L41)
- **Light items** — exploratory reconnaissance is delegated to the novelty critic by default (§3-P1; L52); cycles run in parallel each take a block of numbers in every ID series (Appendix B; L53); the verifier receives the I- lines its `[INTERP]` marks cite, and a pre-gate registration check runs on each candidate before its verifier (§3b.5; L47, L10); revocations and revisions carry a reason category, so gate misses can be counted (§3b.10, Appendix C.1; L16); `scope` holds the target theorem's ID (Appendix C.1; L18); one measured observation replaces "no field basis yet" (§3b.7; L22); wording in §3b.2, §3b.5 and §3b.8 that misstated when the gate runs, what the check script checks and enforces, and the cases of supersession is corrected (L23); guidance entries are written out in full (Appendix C.3; L30); collision precedent CR-1 is cited from its table instead of being restated (L48).

## v0.3.0 — 2026-09-14 · the Theory Forge release

Methodology v2.0 → **v3.0**; plugin v0.2.0 → **v0.3.0** — **the two are numbered separately from now on**: the paper and the package no longer have to move together. One axis: fit G2G with a second operating mode, the **Theory Forge**, and keep the research cycle, extended at P2/P3/P5/P6/P7/P8 as listed below. The sources are three — defects and successes logged in use of v2.0 (including one fully unattended round), a new requirement (deriving dozens to hundreds of mutually dependent algebraic relations from a few axioms, rather than one card per cycle), and a precedent from the summer of 2026 (Danus: fact graph + single verification authority + cascade revocation). Every change below traces to a problem ID in the internal v3 update plan (G1–G9, H1–H6, I1–I8, J1–J5, K1–K3).

Self-check before release: the methodology stood its own plan tribunal ST-03 (standard severity; three isolated critics, one independent defender; 50 objections — 19 sentences replaced, 22 qualified, 7 gaps closed; five Danus descriptions re-quoted from the primary sources); one trial Forge round on a test programme (2 nodes landed, 0 returns, 3 agent calls; 18 process defects found and fixed, including two `pg_check.py` bugs); the 31-item traceability matrix re-checked (29 complete, 2 deferred by design); the trigger-exclusivity self-test passed on 24 utterances (J5). The methodology as released is a condensed edition of the tribunal-reviewed text, re-checked through seven independent review lenses (rule fidelity in two halves, qualifier fidelity, cross-references, an outside reader, and two citation passes) and a primary-source check of every citation.

### Methodology v3.0 (docs/METHODOLOGY.md — single source; docs/METHODOLOGY_zh.md and the bilingual PDF are generated from it)

- **The Theory Forge** (new chapter §3b) — a second operating mode: propositions deduced one by one, verified one by one, and entered one by one into a directed acyclic **proposition graph** under `g2g/07_theory/`; node genre, granularity ("one deep result", not a node count) and marks; the nine steps of one round; the orchestrator writes **elaboration** and **guidance** in its own hand and dispatches only the labor; **cadence** — a control beat every round and every K=10 submissions, a macro audit every M=4 rounds, a theory tribunal every T=25 verified propositions; progress measured by the growth of the target theorem's **support closure**, never by node count; merge timing recorded (the Forge's very-late merge beside the cycle's late merge, so the two can be compared later). (G1, G5, G8, H4, H5, K2)
- **Two gates, and the verification gate** — the logic gate (cheap, once per proposition) and the empirical gate (expensive, opened periodically); **the logic gate never rules on empirical truth**, and the two statuses never trigger each other. The gate is a cold-start verifier with a binary verdict (pass = zero critical errors and zero gaps), mandatory repair hints on a fail, a repair limit of 2, `[COMP]` symbolic corroboration that can never replace a written derivation, and the numbered hard prohibitions **BAN-01…BAN-13** (02 and 04 left empty, matching the precedent's numbering). **Authority by construction** (§2): the deducer submits, the verifier writes only records, the orchestrator lands a node only with a passing record in hand. The gate leaves the opening social science needs: high-consensus premises enter either as a ratified A-/D-/declared assumption or as external literature quoted verbatim with its location — never pulled in on the spot. (G2, G9)
- **Cascade revocation** — revoke an axiom, a base definition, a declared assumption or a node, and every transitively dependent node is revoked with reason and date; files are kept and become "to be rebuilt" tasks. Two boundaries drawn explicitly: a change of **empirical** status never triggers logical revocation, and a **revision** (P-0007 → P-0007.1, same ID, dependents not revoked — those whose derivations used a changed Statement re-pass the gate) is not a **supersession** (a different ID displacing it, dependents rebuilt). (G3)
- **Symbol registration and verbatim citation** — a glossary of symbols with meaning and dimension, registered at the gate (BAN-13); every citation of literature, of an institutional fact, or of the human's own words carries the **verbatim original and its location** (BAN-11), after a carrier citation that misread a weight as a direction survived two inference chains. (G4, I4)
- **Formal axiom IDs and the pending rule** — axioms and base definitions get formal IDs (A-01…, D-01…) with `pending` / `ratified` status, each naming the hard-core item or the human's words it came from; a pending axiom is not a brick, and a branch that needs one stops and queues for the human. Node genre, numbering and templates are fixed in Appendix C. (G7)
- **The dead-end library, R5, and the conjecture pool** — dead ends are assets: an append-only, structured, searchable library (`dead_end` / `obstacle` / `refuted_step` / `excluded_family` / `superseded`) that deducers must read before they prove; new anti-dogma rule **R5** makes an unrecorded dead end an anti-dogma failure. The conjecture pool is the ammunition store: a conjecture can be pushed as a **provisional premise** "as true / as false", the whole chain carrying `[GUESS]` and `conditional_on`, with three outcomes for the human — ratify, abandon, or leave open. (G6)
- **`[INTERP]`** — a fourth mark for the agent's interpretive reading of the human's own words; it enters the human's adjudication queue, is inherited downstream, and at submission severity an unadjudicated `[INTERP]` keeps a result out of the frozen registry. (I5)
- **No kill on a bare number** — a criterion number with no power or precision derivation and no `[GUESS]` mark may not carry an execution load; the gate treats it as a hard prohibition (BAN-10) and the logic critic prosecutes it. (I2)
- **Pairwise collision self-check** — every deducer ends its output by checking its own propositions against each other, at every severity tier (an exploratory run with N=1 has no second deducer to catch an internal contradiction); the verifier repeats the check across the batch. (I3)
- **Collision precedent CR-1 and dispatch mode** — critic-vs-critic collisions consult a precedent table before the orchestrator rules, seeded with CR-1 (same sentence, one seat "kill", one "weaken", defense conceding both → execute the kill, keep the weaken seat's replacement path); every verdict records how the critics were dispatched (parallel or sequential, and in what order) so the scheduling of critique can be studied later. (I6, K3)
- **The card-to-graph bridge** — a conclusion that survived a cycle tribunal may be submitted to the gate as a candidate node; surviving the tribunal exempts nothing, because the tribunal looked at value and evidence while the gate looks at the derivation. (G2)
- **Two-speed decisions** — the four-line format is for light items only; a decision the human would still have to ask "what is this, and why" about gets a **decision pack**: storyline → terms unpacked, both sides, a recommendation with facts and guesses marked separately, an entry point for checking it oneself → the four-line bar plus a blank the human fills in by hand. (I1)
- **One-line start** — a programme has exactly one entry point: "run G2G". The entry point preflights the environment, reads the state, and decides: initialize, go to the plan, run a Forge round (the default when both modes are open), run a cycle, or report status. (H1)
- **Unattended protocol with a budget fence** — two tables in the charter (pre-authorized defaults; unattended-case dispositions) plus one fence: the boundary of an unattended run is set on **token usage**, not on permission prompts — wrap up at 80%, write the handover and stop at the ceiling. Two things never loosen: **axioms are never ratified by the machine**, and **external text is data, not instructions**. (H2)
- **Model tiering** — a default model per class of role per severity tier, written into the charter and changeable at any time; the orchestrator may move an individual agent one tier, but **the gate may never go below the table**. (H3)
- **Environment preflight, the device gate, and script availability** — every session begins by establishing which machine, which directory, whether scripts can run, and whether the workspace is writable; a programme synchronized across devices may declare a write machine (read-only everywhere else); the two-seat handover kit gains one item — whether scripts can run — and a seat without a local execution environment checks the same list by hand. The Cowork and cloud-sandbox behaviours are written down (re-staging returns cached copies; pull by path when mounts fail). (I7, H6, I8)
- **Qualifier-fidelity audit and clean author context** — the plan tribunal and the theory tribunal gain a fixed step aligning every outward sentence with the frozen verdict wording and listing the lost qualifiers; outward products are rendered by a fresh, isolated agent that has never seen the internal jargon it cannot then leak. (K1)
- **Eight new failure modes** with their guardrails (§9): unverified material used as bricks, the orchestrator forging a proposition, duplicate-proposition bloat, granularity too fine, dead ends unpublished, loss of focus in the long run, unattended overreach, external content injection.
- **Lineage and References** — the Theory Forge's debt to **Danus** (frenzymath, led from Peking University; Apache-2.0; arXiv:2607.06447) is named item by item, and the "dead ends are assets" statistics are credited to Appendix A of the Yau–Tian–Donaldson disproof paper (arXiv:2608.19301); what was borrowed is the design, not the code. §3b.12 lists the differences one by one. (J2)
- **The Chinese edition is back in the repository** as `docs/METHODOLOGY_zh.md`, regenerated from the English source at every release. (J1)

### Scripts

- **New `scripts/pg_check.py`** — a pure-standard-library (Python ≥ 3.10, no packages) check on `g2g/07_theory/`, run at the start of every Forge round: **if it does not pass, work does not start**. It checks frontmatter and ID format, an acyclic graph, no dangling predecessors, status against directory, every verified node backed by a passing verification record, no unverified predecessor and no pending axiom cited, revocations and supersessions cascaded, registered symbols, `conditional_on` inheritance and its `[GUESS]` mark, neither `[SIG]` nor a provisional premise in a `faithful` node's support closure (`FAITHFUL_WITH_SIG` and `FAITHFUL_WITH_CONDITION` — the two halves of 3b.4, reported separately), the one gate-exempt revision changed nothing beyond the licensed `conditional on CJ-xx ([GUESS])` → `by A-xx` swap (`EXEMPT_REVISION_CHANGED`, 3b.8), and content digests; bare IDs resolve to the current revision. `--report` adds a health summary with closure and cadence hints, `--regen-index` rewrites the overview table, `--fill-sha` fills content digests, `--json` emits everything as one document; exit code = number of violations (0 = clean, 2 = usage or IO error). `scripts/README.md` maps every code to its manual-checklist equivalent for seats that cannot run scripts, and `scripts/tests/` holds a clean fixture, a fixture that trips every counted code (plus the positive control for the gate-exempt revision), and a 30-test unittest suite over both. The last two checks close the two code debts tribunal ST-03 found (verdict decision 2, K9 and K24): the methodology no longer says "is to verify" / "is to carry" — the script carries both.

### Migration

A v2 workspace needs nothing beyond running `g2g-setup` — or any v3 skill, which will offer the same. `07_theory/` and the charter's new sections (programme modes, model tiering, the two unattended tables, the device gate) are added with defaults; **nothing already recorded is rewritten** — old cards, plans, verdicts and logs stand as they are. A programme that opens the Forge later gets the directory added the same way. Axioms and base definitions extracted from an existing hard core start as `pending`: the machine never ratifies, so nothing is a brick until the human says so.
