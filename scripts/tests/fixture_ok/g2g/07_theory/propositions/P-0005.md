## Statement
Conditional on CJ-01 ([GUESS]): if the peer group of agent i is fixed over the horizon, then r_i is constant over the horizon and ρ_i changes only through x_i.

## Derivation
Step 1: conditional on CJ-01 ([GUESS]), the peer group is fixed. Step 2: by D-01, r_i is the mean consumption of that group; with the group fixed and (by the premise) its consumption profile fixed, r_i is constant. Step 3: by P-0002 (via P-0001), ρ_i = x_i / r_i, so with r_i constant the only channel is x_i.

## Falsification surface
Observable pattern: measured reference levels drifting while group membership is constant would falsify the premise CJ-01.

[GUESS]: rests on CJ-01, an open conjecture; does not count as progress (3b.7). Tagged strengthened rather than faithful because a provisional premise is not a shape present in the ratified axioms (3b.4).
