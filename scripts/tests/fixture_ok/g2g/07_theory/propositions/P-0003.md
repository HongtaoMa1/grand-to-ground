## Statement
Suppose [SIG] U(x_i, r_i) = V(ρ_i) for some differentiable V, with ρ_i = x_i / r_i (P-0001). Then V'(ρ_i) > 0.

## Derivation
Step 1: by the [SIG] shape assumption, ∂U/∂x_i = V'(ρ_i) · ∂ρ_i/∂x_i. Step 2: by P-0001, ∂ρ_i/∂x_i = 1 / r_i > 0 (D-01 via P-0001). Step 3: by A-02, ∂U/∂x_i > 0; dividing by the positive 1 / r_i gives V'(ρ_i) > 0.

## Falsification surface
An agent whose utility rises with x_i while ρ_i is held fixed would falsify the [SIG] shape.

## Notes
[SIG]: the functional form U = V(ρ_i) is not in the axioms; hence tagged strengthened.
